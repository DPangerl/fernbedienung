## Fernbedienung 1.2

Needs **Steckfeld SF1 1.7.0** or newer. More on
[steckfeld.app/fm1-firmware/fernbedienung](https://steckfeld.app/fm1-firmware/fernbedienung).

- **More crew screens:** BUBEN, PRISE, こだま KODAMA, MITSCHNITT and EMPFANG get their own screens. RASTER's modules
  are drawn to DIN as in Steckfeld: SCHLIERE a part drawing, MITSCHNITT a circuit diagram, EMPFANG a polar diagram.
- **KAIRO in Japanese:** KODAMA and MAGE are labelled in Japanese, as in Steckfeld.
- **New macro:** follows Steckfeld's macro (STORE / STEP on REC / SAVE). The screen shows the four encoders side by
  side with their steps stacked upwards, and UNSAVED instead of the blinking ARMED.
