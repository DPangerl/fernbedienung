## Fernbedienung 1.1

Needs **Steckfeld SF1 1.5.0** or newer. More on
[steckfeld.app/fm1-firmware/fernbedienung](https://steckfeld.app/fm1-firmware/fernbedienung).

- **Hacker crews:** VÝPIS, COMBINÉ, 曲げ MAGE and SCHLIERE get the whole screen in their crew's look: the picture,
  the knob tiles, the header and the footer.
- **Screen lag fixed:** while a picture moved, the screen fell further and further behind Steckfeld until it froze.
  It now stays live.
- **Fast knob turns:** knobs report every millisecond, so Steckfeld's acceleration works on quick turns too.

Tested on an FM-1 with an iPad.
