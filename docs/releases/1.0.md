## Fernbedienung 1.0

The first release. Fernbedienung turns the FM-1 into the hardware controller for
[Steckfeld SF1](https://apps.apple.com/app/steckfeld-sf1/id6830018147) on iPad and iPhone. Needs **Steckfeld SF1 1.4.0**.
More on [steckfeld.app/fm1-firmware/fernbedienung](https://steckfeld.app/fm1-firmware/fernbedienung).

- **Audio:** Steckfeld plays through the FM-1 over USB-C; MASTER sets the volume.
- **Keys:** play Steckfeld over USB-MIDI, with OCT−/+, on the FM-1's channel and velocity.
- **Knobs and buttons:** edit the selected module, switch pages and modules, open the patch list, start and stop.
- **Screen:** the selected module with its name, pages, knob values and a picture of it; the cables on the Route
  screen; the patch list, with patches that play a sequence marked SEQ.
- **EDIT:** MIDI channel, velocity and OPEN (Always: Steckfeld opens its programmer on a module picked on the FM-1;
  Never: it stays closed while the FM-1 is connected).
- **Theme:** follows Steckfeld, or light or dark (hold HOME, press EDIT).

Tested on an FM-1 with an iPad. Installs over M-VAVE's V15 and over other community firmware, and back.
