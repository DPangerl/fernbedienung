Shinonome fonts (xfonts-shinonome 0.9.11, bdf/shnmk16.bdf and bdf/shnmk12.bdf)
Original design: Yasuyuki Furukawa, 2000. Maintained by /efont/, The Electronic Font Open Laboratory,
http://openlab.ring.gr.jp/efont/shinonome/ ; archive as distributed by Debian:
https://deb.debian.org/debian/pool/main/x/xfonts-shinonome/xfonts-shinonome_0.9.11.orig.tar.gz

Fernbedienung uses a few kanji and kana from these fonts as 1-bit bitmaps (tools/gen_kanji.py ->
firmware/apps/fernbedienung/fb_kanji.h).
